The problem

Most small teams do not lose sales because the product is bad. They lose sales in the handoff.

Messages pile up. Quote requests arrive without enough details. Someone forgets to follow up. A warm lead goes cold. Nothing gets logged properly. The result is not just “more work” — it is lost revenue.
01

Lead chaos

WhatsApp, Shopee, TikTok and Facebook inquiries live in different inboxes. Teams reply late, miss context, or forget who is ready to buy.

  • Late replies
  • No clear lead status
  • No structured intake
02

Slow quoting

Customers ask for price fast, but teams still need to ask missing questions, check package details, and draft the same answers again and again.

  • Manual quote drafting
  • Repeated FAQ replies
  • Price requests get delayed
03

Follow-up gets dropped

The lead was interested. Nobody followed up. Nobody logged the outcome. Nobody knows if the customer is lost, pending, or ready to convert.

  • No reminders
  • No clean pipeline
  • No deal memory
The solution

A narrow commerce agent for the part of the business that leaks money first.

NeuroArk does not try to become your whole company. It fixes a specific execution layer: inquiry handling, quote drafting, follow-up execution, and sales logging.
⚑

Qualify inquiries

Incoming messages are grouped by buying intent, urgency, and missing information so your team knows what matters first.

✦

Draft replies & quotes

Instead of starting from zero, your team gets ready-to-review reply drafts and quote drafts based on your packages, FAQs, and rules.

↺

Keep follow-up alive

Leads that need a second or third touch do not disappear. The system tracks the status and tells you what should happen next.
